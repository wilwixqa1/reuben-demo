About Ruben

I grew up on Long Island and I've spent my career helping people buy and sell homes in the communities I know best. Central Suffolk County isn't just where I work. It's where I live, where I shop, where I get my hair cut.

I got into real estate because I saw too many agents treat it like a transaction instead of a relationship. When you work with me, you get my cell number, not a call center. I'll tell you what a house is actually worth, even when it's not what you want to hear. And I'll be around long after the closing.

If you're thinking about buying, selling, or just curious what your place is worth, give me a call. No pitch, no pressure.
